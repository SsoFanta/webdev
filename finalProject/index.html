About Lamy's

Lamy's Mexican Grill is at 2235 Santa Clara Drive in Santa Clara, Utah. They sell burritos, tacos, bowls, nachos, enchiladas, salads, quesadillas, drinks, and sides. Their business listing also says they are a healthier alternative to Mexican food.
